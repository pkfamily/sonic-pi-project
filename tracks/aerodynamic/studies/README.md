# Aerodynamic studies

These finite excerpts isolate transcription, sound design, and arrangement
questions without changing an approved full-track version.

## Selected references

- `lead_only.rb` is the canonical corrected mixed-register lead comparison.
- `opening_groove_clean_guitar_multisample.rb` established the sampled guitar
  voice selected for V11.
- `opening_groove_clean_guitar_excerpt.rb` runs the complete twenty-bar opening
  groove with that selected guitar voice.

## Supporting studies

- `layered_lead_builtin.rb` tests built-in synth layering around the approved
  lead without replacing it.

## Rejected or superseded studies

- `opening_groove_pluck.rb` is the initial metallic pluck attempt.
- `opening_groove_additive.rb` and `opening_groove_additive_warm.rb` retain the
  additive alternatives that sounded synthetic or muffled.
- `opening_groove_pulse_hybrid.rb` is the clearer but underpowered pulse/sine
  hybrid.
- `opening_groove_clavinet_multisample.rb` proves the multisample workflow but
  uses the rejected clavinet source.
- `opening_groove_chord_stabs_synth.rb` and
  `opening_groove_chord_stabs_guitar.rb` are the matched stab A/B test; V11
  rejects both stab layers to keep the groove uncluttered.

## Legacy

- `il_macquillage_fixed.rb` preserves an earlier corrected Sonic Pi sketch.

Rendered studies are disposable listening artifacts and belong only under the
ignored `renders/` directory.
